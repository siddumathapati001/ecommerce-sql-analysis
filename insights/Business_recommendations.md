## Business Recommendations & Action Plan

### 1. Improve First-to-Second Purchase Conversion
- Customer retention drops sharply after the first purchase, with only ~5% of customers returning in the next month.
- Focus should be on post-purchase engagement such as targeted offers, follow-up reminders, and loyalty incentives immediately after the first successful delivery.
- Even a small improvement in early retention can significantly increase overall revenue without increasing acquisition costs.

---

### 2. Reduce Revenue Leakage from Non-Delivered Orders
- Approximately ₹0.59M of revenue is associated with non-delivered orders, representing direct revenue exposure.
- Strengthening order validation, seller commitment checks, and faster cancellation handling can help reduce this leakage.
- Monitoring non-delivered orders at seller level will allow early identification of problem sellers.

---

### 3. Address Delivery Delays to Improve Customer Experience
- Around 8% of delivered orders are delayed, and delayed deliveries receive much lower review scores (2.57 vs 4.29).
- Improving delivery accuracy and setting realistic delivery estimates can directly improve customer satisfaction.
- Sellers with consistently high delay rates should be prioritized for logistics audits or support.

---

### 4. Target High-Risk Sellers with Corrective Actions
- Seller risk analysis shows that a small group of sellers account for a disproportionate share of problematic orders.
- Introduce seller-level risk monitoring using metrics like risk score and delay rate.
- High-risk sellers can be given warnings, operational support, or penalties, while reliable sellers can be rewarded with better visibility.

---

### 5. Balance Revenue Scale with Seller Quality
- Some high-revenue sellers also show lower review scores and higher operational issues.
- Revenue contribution should not be the only criterion for seller prioritization.
- Incorporating seller quality metrics alongside revenue can improve long-term customer trust and platform sustainability.

---

### 6. Use Data-Driven Seller Segmentation
- Sellers can be segmented into categories such as high-revenue & low-risk, high-revenue & high-risk, and low-revenue & high-risk.
- This segmentation enables targeted strategies instead of one-size-fits-all seller policies.
- Data-driven segmentation helps reduce operational cost while improving customer experience.
