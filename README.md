# E-Commerce SQL Analysis – Olist Dataset

## Project Overview
This project analyzes an e-commerce platform using the Olist dataset to understand customer behavior, revenue drivers, operational risks, and seller performance. The analysis focuses on identifying key business issues such as low customer retention, revenue leakage, and seller-level operational risk using SQL as the primary tool.

## Objectives
- Validate data integrity and table relationships
- Establish core business metrics such as revenue, AOV, and customer activity
- Analyze customer purchase behavior and retention patterns
- Perform cohort-based retention analysis
- Identify revenue leakage due to delivery failures and delays
- Evaluate seller performance and operational risk
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL (PostgreSQL)
- Jupyter Notebook (supporting analysis)
- Markdown for insights and documentation

## Key Analyses Performed
- Data validation and sanity checks
- Core revenue and order metrics
- One-time vs repeat customer analysis
- Customer cohort and retention analysis
- Revenue leakage due to non-delivered and delayed orders
- Seller performance and risk scoring
- Actionable business recommendations

## Key Findings
- Nearly 97% of customers are one-time buyers, with very low repeat purchase behavior.
- Average Month-1 customer retention is approximately 5%, dropping below 1% beyond two months.
- Around ₹0.59M of revenue is associated with non-delivered orders, representing direct revenue risk.
- Delivery delays significantly reduce customer satisfaction (average rating drops from 4.29 to 2.57).
- Operational risk is concentrated among a small group of sellers with high delay and failure rates.

## Business Impact
The analysis highlights that the business is heavily acquisition-driven, with significant upside in improving early customer retention and reducing seller-level operational risk. Targeted interventions can improve revenue efficiency and customer experience without proportional increases in acquisition spend.

## Dataset
Source: Kaggle – Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset
